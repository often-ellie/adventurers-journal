# Adventurer's Journal

A leather-bound journal for your tabletop character, on your desk. It has pages to write
and draw on, a character sheet, a spellbook, a bestiary and herbarium, maps with pins,
letters and scraps, quests and an inventory.

## Download
Get the newest version from the **[latest release](../../releases/latest)**:
- **Windows:** the file ending in `-Windows-setup.exe`
- **Mac:** the file ending in `-Mac.dmg`

Then follow the **[install guide](INSTALL.md)**. Your computer will warn you once,
because the app isn't from a big company, and the guide shows you how to get past it.

## Updating
Install the newest version the same way. It installs over the old one and keeps your journals.

Your journals stay on your own computer. The app sends nothing anywhere.
